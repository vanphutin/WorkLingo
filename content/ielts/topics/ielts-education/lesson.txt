[[LANGUAGE_BLOCK ielts-education-higher-education]]
expression: higher education
meaning_vi: giáo dục đại học
pronunciation: /ˌhaɪər ˌedʒʊˈkeɪʃən/
collocations: access to higher education
grammar_pattern: Noun phrase: use as a subject, object, or complement after a suitable preposition.
example: Our college wants higher education to be accessible to adults returning after a long break.
common_error: Do not omit education after higher.
[[/LANGUAGE_BLOCK]]

[[LANGUAGE_BLOCK ielts-education-tuition-fees]]
expression: tuition fees
meaning_vi: học phí
pronunciation: /tjuːˈɪʃən fiːz/
collocations: pay tuition fees
grammar_pattern: Noun phrase: use as a subject, object, or complement after a suitable preposition.
example: A new bursary would cover tuition fees for twenty students whose household income is low.
common_error: Use fees in the plural for course charges.
[[/LANGUAGE_BLOCK]]

[[LANGUAGE_BLOCK ielts-education-learning-outcomes]]
expression: learning outcomes
meaning_vi: kết quả học tập dự kiến
pronunciation: /ˈlɜːnɪŋ ˈaʊtkʌmz/
collocations: achieve learning outcomes
grammar_pattern: Noun phrase: use as a subject, object, or complement after a suitable preposition.
example: Teachers will publish learning outcomes before registration so applicants can judge the course's relevance.
common_error: Outcomes are results, not the subjects listed in a timetable.
[[/LANGUAGE_BLOCK]]

[[LANGUAGE_BLOCK ielts-education-critical-thinking]]
expression: critical thinking
meaning_vi: tư duy phản biện
pronunciation: /ˈkrɪtɪkəl ˈθɪŋkɪŋ/
collocations: develop critical thinking
grammar_pattern: Noun phrase: use as a subject, object, or complement after a suitable preposition.
example: Weekly debates will develop critical thinking by asking students to compare evidence from two sources.
common_error: Treat thinking as uncountable here.
[[/LANGUAGE_BLOCK]]

[[LANGUAGE_BLOCK ielts-education-practical-skills]]
expression: practical skills
meaning_vi: kỹ năng thực hành
pronunciation: /ˈpræktɪkəl skɪlz/
collocations: gain practical skills
grammar_pattern: Noun phrase: use as a subject, object, or complement after a suitable preposition.
example: The evening workshop will teach practical skills through repairing donated laptops rather than watching demonstrations.
common_error: Practical means useful in action; it differs from practicable.
[[/LANGUAGE_BLOCK]]

[[LANGUAGE_BLOCK ielts-education-independent-study]]
expression: independent study
meaning_vi: việc tự học
pronunciation: /ˌɪndɪˈpendənt ˈstʌdi/
collocations: time for independent study
grammar_pattern: Noun phrase: use as a subject, object, or complement after a suitable preposition.
example: The library will reserve quiet desks for independent study, while group meetings stay upstairs.
common_error: Use study without an article when referring to the activity.
[[/LANGUAGE_BLOCK]]

[[LANGUAGE_BLOCK ielts-education-academic-performance]]
expression: academic performance
meaning_vi: kết quả học tập
pronunciation: /ˌækəˈdemɪk pəˈfɔːməns/
collocations: improve academic performance
grammar_pattern: Noun phrase: use as a subject, object, or complement after a suitable preposition.
example: Tutors will discuss academic performance privately instead of ranking students on a public noticeboard.
common_error: Do not confuse performance with attendance.
[[/LANGUAGE_BLOCK]]

[[LANGUAGE_BLOCK ielts-education-equal-opportunities]]
expression: equal opportunities
meaning_vi: cơ hội bình đẳng
pronunciation: /ˈiːkwəl ˌɒpəˈtjuːnətiz/
collocations: promote equal opportunities
grammar_pattern: Noun phrase: use as a subject, object, or complement after a suitable preposition.
example: To promote equal opportunities, application forms will be available in print as well as online.
common_error: Use opportunities, not opportunites.
[[/LANGUAGE_BLOCK]]

[[LANGUAGE_BLOCK ielts-education-lifelong-learning]]
expression: lifelong learning
meaning_vi: học tập suốt đời
pronunciation: /ˌlaɪflɒŋ ˈlɜːnɪŋ/
collocations: encourage lifelong learning
grammar_pattern: Noun phrase: use as a subject, object, or complement after a suitable preposition.
example: An open lecture each month will encourage lifelong learning without requiring students to complete a qualification.
common_error: Use lifelong as one word before learning.
[[/LANGUAGE_BLOCK]]

[[LANGUAGE_BLOCK ielts-education-vocational-training]]
expression: vocational training
meaning_vi: đào tạo nghề
pronunciation: /vəʊˈkeɪʃənəl ˈtreɪnɪŋ/
collocations: provide vocational training
grammar_pattern: Noun phrase: use as a subject, object, or complement after a suitable preposition.
example: Local employers will offer vocational training placements, but participation will remain optional.
common_error: Training is normally uncountable; avoid a training here.
[[/LANGUAGE_BLOCK]]

[[LANGUAGE_BLOCK ielts-education-student-engagement]]
expression: student engagement
meaning_vi: sự tham gia tích cực của người học
pronunciation: /ˈstjuːdənt ɪnˈɡeɪdʒmənt/
collocations: increase student engagement
grammar_pattern: Noun phrase: use as a subject, object, or complement after a suitable preposition.
example: The committee will measure student engagement through participation and feedback rather than login counts alone.
common_error: Engagement describes involvement, not simply being present.
[[/LANGUAGE_BLOCK]]

[[LANGUAGE_BLOCK ielts-education-assessment-criteria]]
expression: assessment criteria
meaning_vi: tiêu chí đánh giá
pronunciation: /əˈsesmənt kraɪˈtɪəriə/
collocations: transparent assessment criteria
grammar_pattern: Noun phrase: use as a subject, object, or complement after a suitable preposition.
example: Published assessment criteria will explain how evidence, organization and accuracy contribute to each assignment.
common_error: Criteria is plural; its singular form is criterion.
[[/LANGUAGE_BLOCK]]

[CONTENT ielts-education-proposal]
type: email
text:
<<<
Dear planning group,
We are a college committee planning support for adult students. Please compare the priorities below before our next meeting.
Our college wants higher education to be accessible to adults returning after a long break.
A new bursary would cover tuition fees for twenty students whose household income is low.
Teachers will publish learning outcomes before registration so applicants can judge the course's relevance.
Weekly debates will develop critical thinking by asking students to compare evidence from two sources.
The evening workshop will teach practical skills through repairing donated laptops rather than watching demonstrations.
The library will reserve quiet desks for independent study, while group meetings stay upstairs.
Tutors will discuss academic performance privately instead of ranking students on a public noticeboard.
To promote equal opportunities, application forms will be available in print as well as online.
An open lecture each month will encourage lifelong learning without requiring students to complete a qualification.
Local employers will offer vocational training placements, but participation will remain optional.
The committee will measure student engagement through participation and feedback rather than login counts alone.
Published assessment criteria will explain how evidence, organization and accuracy contribute to each assignment.
Please recommend one change that we can explain and evaluate, while acknowledging what this proposal does not yet establish.
Regards, the planning coordinator
>>>
[/CONTENT]

[CONTENT ielts-education-discussion]
type: dialogue
text:
<<<
An (nam): Let us review this proposal from the perspective of people who will actually use it.
Lan (nữ): I agree. We should compare the intended benefits with practical barriers and decide what to ask the planning group.
An: I left higher education ten years ago, so I would welcome a short introduction before enrolling.
Lan: Lower tuition fees would help me, but I also need to know the cost of books.
An: Clear learning outcomes would let me compare a digital course with a business course.
Lan: I want critical thinking practice because advertisements often make claims without explaining their evidence.
An: Could the practical skills workshop run on Saturday, when I do not have a work shift?
Lan: I can do independent study at the library, but I cannot always find a quiet place at home.
An: My academic performance improved when a tutor explained my mistakes instead of only giving a mark.
Lan: Equal opportunities should include an accessible classroom, not only an identical application form.
An: For me, lifelong learning means continuing to ask questions after my formal course ends.
Lan: Vocational training appeals to my brother because he wants supervised experience in a real workshop.
An: Student engagement might rise if learners could suggest examples drawn from their own jobs.
Lan: Please show us the assessment criteria early so we can plan our work rather than guess what matters.
An: We have several reasonable priorities, but the budget cannot cover every suggestion immediately.
Lan: Let us recommend one change, explain our reasons, and identify evidence that would tell us whether it worked.
>>>
[/CONTENT]
