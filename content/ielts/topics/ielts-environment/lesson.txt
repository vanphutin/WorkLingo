[[LANGUAGE_BLOCK ielts-environment-carbon-emissions]]
expression: carbon emissions
meaning_vi: lượng khí thải carbon
pronunciation: /ˈkɑːbən ɪˈmɪʃənz/
collocations: reduce carbon emissions
grammar_pattern: Noun phrase: use as a subject, object, or complement after a suitable preposition.
example: The council will compare carbon emissions from its vehicles before deciding which ones to replace.
common_error: Use emissions in the plural for released gases.
[[/LANGUAGE_BLOCK]]

[[LANGUAGE_BLOCK ielts-environment-renewable-energy]]
expression: renewable energy
meaning_vi: năng lượng tái tạo
pronunciation: /rɪˈnjuːəbəl ˈenədʒi/
collocations: invest in renewable energy
grammar_pattern: Noun phrase: use as a subject, object, or complement after a suitable preposition.
example: A proposed roof installation would supply renewable energy to the community hall during daylight hours.
common_error: Energy is uncountable in this phrase.
[[/LANGUAGE_BLOCK]]

[[LANGUAGE_BLOCK ielts-environment-waste-management]]
expression: waste management
meaning_vi: quản lý chất thải
pronunciation: /weɪst ˈmænɪdʒmənt/
collocations: effective waste management
grammar_pattern: Noun phrase: use as a subject, object, or complement after a suitable preposition.
example: The waste management contract will require separate collection of food scraps from the market.
common_error: Waste is a noun modifier; do not pluralize it here.
[[/LANGUAGE_BLOCK]]

[[LANGUAGE_BLOCK ielts-environment-biodiversity-loss]]
expression: biodiversity loss
meaning_vi: sự suy giảm đa dạng sinh học
pronunciation: /ˌbaɪəʊdaɪˈvɜːsəti lɒs/
collocations: prevent biodiversity loss
grammar_pattern: Noun phrase: use as a subject, object, or complement after a suitable preposition.
example: Surveyors will assess biodiversity loss along the river before any further paths are built.
common_error: Loss is the noun; lose is a verb.
[[/LANGUAGE_BLOCK]]

[[LANGUAGE_BLOCK ielts-environment-public-transport]]
expression: public transport
meaning_vi: giao thông công cộng
pronunciation: /ˌpʌblɪk ˈtrænspɔːt/
collocations: improve public transport
grammar_pattern: Noun phrase: use as a subject, object, or complement after a suitable preposition.
example: The budget proposal extends public transport on Sunday mornings rather than adding a new car park.
common_error: Transport is normally uncountable in British English.
[[/LANGUAGE_BLOCK]]

[[LANGUAGE_BLOCK ielts-environment-energy-efficiency]]
expression: energy efficiency
meaning_vi: hiệu quả sử dụng năng lượng
pronunciation: /ˈenədʒi ɪˈfɪʃənsi/
collocations: improve energy efficiency
grammar_pattern: Noun phrase: use as a subject, object, or complement after a suitable preposition.
example: An energy efficiency audit found that sealing windows should come before replacing the hall's heating system.
common_error: Efficiency is a noun; efficient is an adjective.
[[/LANGUAGE_BLOCK]]

[[LANGUAGE_BLOCK ielts-environment-natural-habitats]]
expression: natural habitats
meaning_vi: môi trường sống tự nhiên
pronunciation: /ˈnætʃərəl ˈhæbɪtæts/
collocations: protect natural habitats
grammar_pattern: Noun phrase: use as a subject, object, or complement after a suitable preposition.
example: Volunteers will mark natural habitats on a map so maintenance crews can avoid disturbing them.
common_error: Use habitats for different places where species live.
[[/LANGUAGE_BLOCK]]

[[LANGUAGE_BLOCK ielts-environment-single-use-plastic]]
expression: single-use plastic
meaning_vi: nhựa dùng một lần
pronunciation: /ˌsɪŋɡəl juːs ˈplæstɪk/
collocations: reduce single-use plastic
grammar_pattern: Noun phrase: use as a subject, object, or complement after a suitable preposition.
example: Market stalls will trial reusable cups to reduce single-use plastic, with a refundable deposit on each cup.
common_error: Plastic is a material noun in this use.
[[/LANGUAGE_BLOCK]]

[[LANGUAGE_BLOCK ielts-environment-sustainable-consumption]]
expression: sustainable consumption
meaning_vi: tiêu dùng bền vững
pronunciation: /səˈsteɪnəbəl kənˈsʌmpʃən/
collocations: encourage sustainable consumption
grammar_pattern: Noun phrase: use as a subject, object, or complement after a suitable preposition.
example: A repair fair will encourage sustainable consumption by helping residents keep useful appliances for longer.
common_error: Consumption is the noun, not consume.
[[/LANGUAGE_BLOCK]]

[[LANGUAGE_BLOCK ielts-environment-environmental-awareness]]
expression: environmental awareness
meaning_vi: nhận thức về môi trường
pronunciation: /ɪnˌvaɪrənˈmentəl əˈweənəs/
collocations: raise environmental awareness
grammar_pattern: Noun phrase: use as a subject, object, or complement after a suitable preposition.
example: School visits will build environmental awareness through a river survey rather than a poster competition alone.
common_error: Use raise awareness, not rise awareness.
[[/LANGUAGE_BLOCK]]

[[LANGUAGE_BLOCK ielts-environment-climate-adaptation]]
expression: climate adaptation
meaning_vi: thích ứng với khí hậu
pronunciation: /ˈklaɪmət ˌædæpˈteɪʃən/
collocations: plan climate adaptation
grammar_pattern: Noun phrase: use as a subject, object, or complement after a suitable preposition.
example: The climate adaptation plan prioritizes shaded waiting areas for residents during unusually hot weather.
common_error: Adaptation differs from mitigation, which addresses causes.
[[/LANGUAGE_BLOCK]]

[[LANGUAGE_BLOCK ielts-environment-resource-conservation]]
expression: resource conservation
meaning_vi: bảo tồn tài nguyên
pronunciation: /rɪˈsɔːs ˌkɒnsəˈveɪʃən/
collocations: support resource conservation
grammar_pattern: Noun phrase: use as a subject, object, or complement after a suitable preposition.
example: Monthly water-meter readings will show whether the resource conservation campaign changes actual use.
common_error: Resource modifies conservation and stays singular here.
[[/LANGUAGE_BLOCK]]

[CONTENT ielts-environment-proposal]
type: email
text:
<<<
Dear planning group,
We are a town deciding how to use a limited environmental budget. Please compare the priorities below before our next meeting.
The council will compare carbon emissions from its vehicles before deciding which ones to replace.
A proposed roof installation would supply renewable energy to the community hall during daylight hours.
The waste management contract will require separate collection of food scraps from the market.
Surveyors will assess biodiversity loss along the river before any further paths are built.
The budget proposal extends public transport on Sunday mornings rather than adding a new car park.
An energy efficiency audit found that sealing windows should come before replacing the hall's heating system.
Volunteers will mark natural habitats on a map so maintenance crews can avoid disturbing them.
Market stalls will trial reusable cups to reduce single-use plastic, with a refundable deposit on each cup.
A repair fair will encourage sustainable consumption by helping residents keep useful appliances for longer.
School visits will build environmental awareness through a river survey rather than a poster competition alone.
The climate adaptation plan prioritizes shaded waiting areas for residents during unusually hot weather.
Monthly water-meter readings will show whether the resource conservation campaign changes actual use.
Please recommend one change that we can explain and evaluate, while acknowledging what this proposal does not yet establish.
Regards, the planning coordinator
>>>
[/CONTENT]

[CONTENT ielts-environment-discussion]
type: dialogue
text:
<<<
An (nam): Let us review this proposal from the perspective of people who will actually use it.
Lan (nữ): I agree. We should compare the intended benefits with practical barriers and decide what to ask the planning group.
An: Could we publish carbon emissions per journey as well as the annual total, so different routes can be compared?
Lan: Renewable energy on the hall roof sounds promising, but we should check maintenance costs before voting.
An: Better waste management would help our market because mixed rubbish currently makes the recycling bins unusable.
Lan: I am concerned about biodiversity loss because the new path could remove nesting areas as well as weeds.
An: Public transport would be more useful to my family if buses ran early enough for weekend work.
Lan: Energy efficiency improvements might reduce bills sooner than an expensive replacement that we cannot yet afford.
An: Protecting natural habitats needs a clear map; a sign saying be careful is too vague for contractors.
Lan: A reduction in single-use plastic is worthwhile only if customers can easily return and reuse the cups.
An: Sustainable consumption should include repairing what we own rather than buying a new product with a green label.
Lan: Environmental awareness may improve when children see the local river themselves and discuss what they find.
An: Climate adaptation must consider older residents who cannot comfortably walk to a distant shaded park.
Lan: To assess resource conservation, we need water readings from comparable months, not only a count of leaflets.
An: We have several reasonable priorities, but the budget cannot cover every suggestion immediately.
Lan: Let us recommend one change, explain our reasons, and identify evidence that would tell us whether it worked.
>>>
[/CONTENT]
