[[LANGUAGE_BLOCK ielts-culture-traditions-cultural-heritage]]
expression: cultural heritage
meaning_vi: di sản văn hóa
pronunciation: /ˈkʌltʃərəl ˈherɪtɪdʒ/
collocations: preserve cultural heritage
grammar_pattern: Noun phrase: use as a subject, object, or complement after a suitable preposition.
example: Our cultural heritage project will record residents' accounts with permission rather than treat museum labels as the only authority.
common_error: Heritage is generally uncountable when naming shared traditions.
[[/LANGUAGE_BLOCK]]

[[LANGUAGE_BLOCK ielts-culture-traditions-local-traditions]]
expression: local traditions
meaning_vi: truyền thống địa phương
pronunciation: /ˈləʊkəl trəˈdɪʃənz/
collocations: maintain local traditions
grammar_pattern: Noun phrase: use as a subject, object, or complement after a suitable preposition.
example: The festival will present local traditions through demonstrations led by people who practise them.
common_error: Use traditions for distinct inherited practices.
[[/LANGUAGE_BLOCK]]

[[LANGUAGE_BLOCK ielts-culture-traditions-traditional-crafts]]
expression: traditional crafts
meaning_vi: nghề thủ công truyền thống
pronunciation: /trəˈdɪʃənəl krɑːfts/
collocations: learn traditional crafts
grammar_pattern: Noun phrase: use as a subject, object, or complement after a suitable preposition.
example: Workshops on traditional crafts will pay makers for their preparation time as well as the public demonstration.
common_error: Crafts can name different skilled activities, not only finished products.
[[/LANGUAGE_BLOCK]]

[[LANGUAGE_BLOCK ielts-culture-traditions-cultural-diversity]]
expression: cultural diversity
meaning_vi: sự đa dạng văn hóa
pronunciation: /ˈkʌltʃərəl daɪˈvɜːsəti/
collocations: celebrate cultural diversity
grammar_pattern: Noun phrase: use as a subject, object, or complement after a suitable preposition.
example: The planning group will reflect cultural diversity by inviting recent arrivals to help choose the programme.
common_error: Diversity is a noun; diverse is an adjective.
[[/LANGUAGE_BLOCK]]

[[LANGUAGE_BLOCK ielts-culture-traditions-shared-identity]]
expression: shared identity
meaning_vi: bản sắc chung
pronunciation: /ʃeəd aɪˈdentəti/
collocations: a sense of shared identity
grammar_pattern: Noun phrase: use as a subject, object, or complement after a suitable preposition.
example: A discussion on shared identity will welcome disagreement instead of requiring everyone to describe the town in the same way.
common_error: Shared means held in common; do not confuse it with identical.
[[/LANGUAGE_BLOCK]]

[[LANGUAGE_BLOCK ielts-culture-traditions-oral-history]]
expression: oral history
meaning_vi: lịch sử truyền khẩu
pronunciation: /ˌɔːrəl ˈhɪstəri/
collocations: record oral history
grammar_pattern: Noun phrase: use as a subject, object, or complement after a suitable preposition.
example: Every oral history contributor can review their recording before deciding whether it may be displayed.
common_error: Oral refers to spoken accounts; aural relates to hearing.
[[/LANGUAGE_BLOCK]]

[[LANGUAGE_BLOCK ielts-culture-traditions-community-participation]]
expression: community participation
meaning_vi: sự tham gia của cộng đồng
pronunciation: /kəˈmjuːnəti pɑːˌtɪsɪˈpeɪʃən/
collocations: encourage community participation
grammar_pattern: Noun phrase: use as a subject, object, or complement after a suitable preposition.
example: Community participation will include telephone suggestions for residents who cannot attend the evening meeting.
common_error: Participation is followed by in when naming the activity.
[[/LANGUAGE_BLOCK]]

[[LANGUAGE_BLOCK ielts-culture-traditions-historic-buildings]]
expression: historic buildings
meaning_vi: các tòa nhà có ý nghĩa lịch sử
pronunciation: /hɪˈstɒrɪk ˈbɪldɪŋz/
collocations: protect historic buildings
grammar_pattern: Noun phrase: use as a subject, object, or complement after a suitable preposition.
example: A guided walk will discuss historic buildings without entering private homes or blocking residents' doorways.
common_error: Historic means historically significant; historical means related to history.
[[/LANGUAGE_BLOCK]]

[[LANGUAGE_BLOCK ielts-culture-traditions-artistic-expression]]
expression: artistic expression
meaning_vi: sự biểu đạt nghệ thuật
pronunciation: /ɑːˈtɪstɪk ɪkˈspreʃən/
collocations: support artistic expression
grammar_pattern: Noun phrase: use as a subject, object, or complement after a suitable preposition.
example: An open stage will support artistic expression, with clear time limits applied equally to all performers.
common_error: Expression is a noun; express is the verb.
[[/LANGUAGE_BLOCK]]

[[LANGUAGE_BLOCK ielts-culture-traditions-cultural-exchange]]
expression: cultural exchange
meaning_vi: giao lưu văn hóa
pronunciation: /ˈkʌltʃərəl ɪksˈtʃeɪndʒ/
collocations: encourage cultural exchange
grammar_pattern: Noun phrase: use as a subject, object, or complement after a suitable preposition.
example: The cultural exchange table will pair demonstrations with questions so visitors can learn without treating makers as exhibits.
common_error: Exchange implies interaction rather than one-way display.
[[/LANGUAGE_BLOCK]]

[[LANGUAGE_BLOCK ielts-culture-traditions-authentic-experience]]
expression: authentic experience
meaning_vi: trải nghiệm chân thực
pronunciation: /ɔːˈθentɪk ɪkˈspɪəriəns/
collocations: an authentic experience
grammar_pattern: Noun phrase: use as a subject, object, or complement after a suitable preposition.
example: The brochure will not promise a single authentic experience because residents interpret the same tradition differently.
common_error: Use an before authentic experience.
[[/LANGUAGE_BLOCK]]

[[LANGUAGE_BLOCK ielts-culture-traditions-intergenerational-learning]]
expression: intergenerational learning
meaning_vi: học hỏi giữa các thế hệ
pronunciation: /ˌɪntəˌdʒenəˈreɪʃənəl ˈlɜːnɪŋ/
collocations: support intergenerational learning
grammar_pattern: Noun phrase: use as a subject, object, or complement after a suitable preposition.
example: A repair workshop will support intergenerational learning by inviting older makers and younger digital designers to solve a problem together.
common_error: Intergenerational describes exchange across generations.
[[/LANGUAGE_BLOCK]]

[CONTENT ielts-culture-traditions-proposal]
type: email
text:
<<<
Dear planning group,
We are a museum and residents planning a small community festival. Please compare the priorities below before our next meeting.
Our cultural heritage project will record residents' accounts with permission rather than treat museum labels as the only authority.
The festival will present local traditions through demonstrations led by people who practise them.
Workshops on traditional crafts will pay makers for their preparation time as well as the public demonstration.
The planning group will reflect cultural diversity by inviting recent arrivals to help choose the programme.
A discussion on shared identity will welcome disagreement instead of requiring everyone to describe the town in the same way.
Every oral history contributor can review their recording before deciding whether it may be displayed.
Community participation will include telephone suggestions for residents who cannot attend the evening meeting.
A guided walk will discuss historic buildings without entering private homes or blocking residents' doorways.
An open stage will support artistic expression, with clear time limits applied equally to all performers.
The cultural exchange table will pair demonstrations with questions so visitors can learn without treating makers as exhibits.
The brochure will not promise a single authentic experience because residents interpret the same tradition differently.
A repair workshop will support intergenerational learning by inviting older makers and younger digital designers to solve a problem together.
Please recommend one change that we can explain and evaluate, while acknowledging what this proposal does not yet establish.
Regards, the planning coordinator
>>>
[/CONTENT]

[CONTENT ielts-culture-traditions-discussion]
type: dialogue
text:
<<<
An (nam): Let us review this proposal from the perspective of people who will actually use it.
Lan (nữ): I agree. We should compare the intended benefits with practical barriers and decide what to ask the planning group.
An: Cultural heritage includes everyday skills my grandmother uses, not only objects kept behind glass.
Lan: Local traditions can change, so younger participants should be able to explain how they adapt an old practice.
An: Traditional crafts deserve respect for the time involved; a maker should not be expected to work for exposure alone.
Lan: Cultural diversity means listening to people who live here now rather than presenting one group as a curiosity.
An: Shared identity should leave room for different experiences, because belonging does not require identical memories.
Lan: Oral history contributors should be able to withdraw a recording if they later feel uncomfortable about public access.
An: Community participation would improve if the meeting time did not exclude parents who collect children from school.
Lan: Historic buildings can be interesting from the street, and visitors still need to respect the people living inside.
An: Artistic expression should include new work, not only performances that match visitors' expectations of tradition.
Lan: Cultural exchange works when both sides can ask questions rather than one group doing all the explaining.
An: An authentic experience for me would involve an honest conversation, including things that do not fit a tourist brochure.
Lan: Intergenerational learning can work in both directions when an older craftsperson and a younger designer each share useful knowledge.
An: We have several reasonable priorities, but the budget cannot cover every suggestion immediately.
Lan: Let us recommend one change, explain our reasons, and identify evidence that would tell us whether it worked.
>>>
[/CONTENT]
